The model runs on your machine, next to CS2, so it is competing with the game for memory. A 1-3B model answers chat perfectly well and runs on a 4GB card - or on no GPU at all. Bigger is funnier, not necessary.

Options

Listening

?

The bot listens to what your speakers are playing and transcribes it with a local Whisper model, so it hears the same voice comms you do without touching the game or your microphone. It hears all of the game - gunfire included - and the mix carries no names, so transcripts are rougher than chat and nobody is quoted by name. Answers always go to team chat, because voice comms are team-only.

GSI reports coordinates, not callout names, so you teach it: stand somewhere in game, type the name and record it. Anything within ~400 units of a recorded point resolves to that name. If CS2 is not reporting a position, the bot falls back to health and bomb state.
